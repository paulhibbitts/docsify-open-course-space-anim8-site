# Digital Animation – Moved to Docsify-This

> The Canvas Commons course GPV – Digital Animation, brought into Grav Open Course Space from its Common Cartridge export by the [Common Cartridge Converter](https://common-cartridge-converter.hibbittsdesign.org), then moved to [Docsify-This](https://docsify-this.net/#/) as plain Markdown pages by the [Open Publishing Course Space Bridge](https://open-publishing-course-space-bridge.hibbittsdesign.org).

View it as a website: https://docsify-this.net/?basePath=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/paulhibbitts/docsify-open-course-space-anim8-site/main&homepage=home.md&sidebar=true&loadSidebar=_sidebar.md&loadNavbar=_navbar.md&pagination=true&searchbox=true&collapsibleSidebarGroups=true&dark-mode=auto&name=Digital%20Animation

These pages are the bridge's output as it is, shown like the course in Open Course Space: the home page starts with the course's banner and welcome, then a card for each of its pages. The navbar links to the Modules, Essentials, Resources and Syllabus pages, the sidebar lists the modules and their pages, and Previous/Next buttons follow the pages in order.

The original course is by D. Ingram, licensed under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Its Grav version is at https://demo.hibbittsdesign.org/variants/grav-open-course-space-common-cartridge-it2dda/anim8 – this version uses the same content:

- `home.md` – the course's home page, with `home-*.md` for the pages shown as cards
- `modules.md` – a card for each module
- `module-1-basic-2d-objects.md` … `module-3-final-project.md` and `file-management.md` – each module's overview
- `module-01-01-*.md` … – the pages inside each module
- `essentials.md`, `resources.md`, `syllabus.md` – the course's other pages
- `images/` – the course's images

Attached files such as PDFs weren't carried over.

The pages are clean Markdown, without the bridge's hidden notes. The bridge can still turn them into an Open Course Space course, like any Docsify site, but not exactly.
